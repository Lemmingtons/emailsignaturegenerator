Privacy & Terms

Last updated: 28 April 2026

What we store

The signature generator runs entirely in your browser. The form fields you fill in (name, title, contact details, social links, colour preferences) never leave your device unless you copy or download your signature. We do not have user accounts.

Pro purchases

Pro is sold as a one-time payment via Stripe. We receive a Stripe checkout session ID and your Stripe customer ID, which we use to issue a signed access token (JWT) that is stored in your browser's localStorage. We do not see or store your card details — Stripe handles all payment information.

Hosted photo upload (Pro)

When you upload a photo as a Pro user, we store the image on Cloudflare R2 keyed to your Stripe customer ID and serve it from emailsignaturegenerator.ai/u/…. Before upload, your browser resizes the image to 400×400 pixels and re-encodes it as JPEG, which strips EXIF metadata (including any embedded location data).

Only your most recent uploaded photo is retained — re-uploading replaces the previous file. Hosted photos are publicly accessible by URL (this is intentional, so any recipient's email client can fetch them).

Acceptable use. Uploaded images must be your own content or content you are licensed to use. Prohibited content — illegal material, content that infringes others' rights, or child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — will be removed and the associated Pro access may be terminated without refund. We may report illegal content to law enforcement.

Deletion. To delete your hosted photo, email info@emailsignaturegenerator.ai from the address associated with your Stripe purchase, or simply re-upload to overwrite the previous file.

Cookies and analytics

We use only first-party storage (localStorage) to remember your Pro access token. We do not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Refunds

Because Pro is a one-time digital purchase that grants immediate access to all templates and hosted photo storage, refunds are handled case-by-case. Email info@emailsignaturegenerator.ai within 14 days of purchase if Pro doesn't work for you.

Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or content reports: info@emailsignaturegenerator.ai.
