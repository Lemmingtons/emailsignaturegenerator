Frequently asked questions

Does the CTA Box template work in Gmail and Outlook?

Yes. The CTA Box template is table-based HTML with inline styles, designed for Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and Yahoo Mail. Rendering varies by client, so send yourself a test email after installing it.

Can I use the CTA Box template without a photo?

Yes. Leave the photo empty and the layout closes up around your details, as in the second example on this page.

How much does the CTA Box template cost?

Building and previewing it is free with no account. Copying the finished signature into your email client costs US$9 once, which unlocks all 28 templates. There is no subscription.
