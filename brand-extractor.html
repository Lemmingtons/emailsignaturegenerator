Website Brand Colour and Logo Extractor

Type any website and get its logo, brand colours as hex codes, company name, phone number and social links. Free, with no account.

How it works

  1. We fetch the homepage you type, the way a browser would on first load.
  2. The name comes from the site's structured data or its social sharing tags. The logo comes from structured data, logo images or the site icon.
  3. Colours come from the theme colour, the web app manifest and a ranking of the colours used in the page's CSS, ignoring greys.
  4. The phone number comes from structured data or the first tap-to-call link, and social links from links to LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and Google reviews.

In our test of 12 public websites on 25 October 2026, every lookup answered within 2.6 seconds, with a median under half a second. It found a logo on 10 of the 12 sites and brand colours on 9. Sites that block automated visits, or draw their branding with JavaScript, return less.

What happens to the address you type

The address is sent to our server so it can read the public page. Nothing is stored, and the logo is returned to your browser rather than saved on our site. Only public pages on standard web ports can be read. See Privacy & Terms.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the brand colours of a website?

Type the website address above. The extractor reads the homepage, checks the theme colour, web app manifest and the colours used in the site's CSS, and returns the two most likely brand colours as hex codes you can copy.

How do I download a logo from a website?

Type the website address and, if a logo is found, click Download logo. The extractor checks the site's structured data, logo images and icons. Only download logos you have the right to use, such as your own company's.

Why did it not find a logo or colours?

Some sites block automated visits, and some draw their logo or set their colours with JavaScript after the page loads. The extractor reads the page as delivered, so those sites return less. You can still enter the details by hand in the generator.

Is the brand extractor free?

Yes. It is free with no account, and nothing you look up is stored. Lookups are rate limited to stop abuse.

What can I do with the results?

Copy the hex codes into your design tools, or click Make a signature with this brand to open the email signature generator with the logo, colours and links already filled in.
