How to choose an email signature template

  • Start with the next step. If you want meetings booked, use a Sales template with a button. If clients phone you, use a layout that puts the phone first, such as Trades.
  • Decide on a photo. A headshot helps people who have not met you. If you do not want one, Monogram, Minimal, Clean, Dash and Text Only look finished without it.
  • Match your brand. Templates with an accent rule or bar carry your brand colour without needing a logo. Branded and Letterhead are built around the company name or logo.
  • Think about long threads. Short, wide layouts such as Classic, Clean and Boardroom take up less space when every reply repeats your signature.
  • Test before you roll it out. Install it, send yourself an email and check it on a phone and in dark mode. The free signature health check flags the common problems.

Frequently asked questions

Are these email signature templates free?

You can open any of the 28 templates, fill in your details and preview it free, with no account. Copying the finished signature into Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail costs US$9 once. There is no subscription and no branding on your signature.

Which email signature template works best in Outlook?

Every template uses table-based HTML with inline styles, which is what Outlook needs. The safest choices for classic Outlook on Windows are the layouts without gradients or rounded panels, such as Classic, Executive, Corporate, Minimal and Text Only. Always send yourself a test email after installing.

How long can an email signature be in Gmail?

Gmail limits a signature to 10,000 characters, including the HTML. Each template page lists the size of its HTML with sample details so you can see how much room is left. Hosted images count as a short link, not as the image itself.

Can I use my own logo and brand colours in a template?

Yes. Type your company website in the generator and it looks for your logo, brand colours, company name, phone number and social links, then applies them to whichever template you choose. You can also set every colour by hand.

What is the difference between a template and an example?

A template is a layout the generator can fill with your details. Examples show finished signatures for particular jobs and situations. This page lists the 28 templates; the examples page shows 52 finished signatures and which template each one starts from.
