Frequently asked questions

Where should I start?

Read the best practices guide to decide what goes in your signature, then follow the guide for your email client to install it. If you already have a signature, run it through the free health check first.

How do I install a signature in Gmail or Outlook?

Copy the signature, then paste it into Gmail's Signature box under Settings, See all settings, General, or into Outlook's signature settings. Set it as the default for new emails and for replies. The Gmail and Outlook guides cover each version step by step.

Why does my signature look different on someone else's screen?

Email clients render HTML differently, block images until the reader allows them, and apply dark mode in their own way. Table-based layouts with hosted images hold up best. The size and logo guides explain the settings that matter.
