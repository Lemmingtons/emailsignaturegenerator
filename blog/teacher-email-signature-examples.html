Teacher Email Signature Examples: 8 Templates for Schools

By Email Signature Generator · Published October 2026 · 8 min read

The short answer: a teacher email signature needs your name as parents and students use it (for example Ms. Patel), your subject or grade, your school, and the best way and time to reach you. Add a school phone number or office hours if your school uses them. Leave out your personal phone number and anything your school's communication policy does not allow.

Teachers email a wider mix of people than almost anyone: parents, students, colleagues, administrators and outside agencies. A good teacher signature answers the question each of them has, which is usually "who is this, which class is it about, and how do I reach them?" It should do that without giving away a personal phone number or promising replies at all hours.

Check your district or school policy first. Many schools set rules on signatures, logos, quotes and the use of personal contact details. Where a policy exists, it wins over any template here.

What teachers should leave out

  • A personal mobile number. Once it is in a signature it is in every forwarded email. Use the school office number or an extension.
  • Inspirational quotes. Many school policies discourage them, and they add length to every reply.
  • Personal social media. Link only to official class or school accounts.
  • Unapproved logos. Use the school's approved logo file or none.
  • A long list of qualifications. One relevant credential, if any, is plenty. Our credentials guide covers what to list.
  • Details only in an image. Many school and parent inboxes block images, so keep your name and contact details as text.

Teacher signature design tips

  • Keep it to four or five lines. Parents read most school email on their phones.
  • Use one font, 12 to 14px, and your school colour as the only accent.
  • If you add a logo, keep it small: about 120 to 160px wide. See our email signature size guide.
  • Use a shorter signature for replies, or none, so long parent threads stay readable.
  • Send a test to a personal Gmail address and open it on a phone before the term starts.

How to add your signature

Most schools use Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. In Gmail, open Settings, then See all settings, and paste your signature into the Signature box. In Outlook, open Settings, then Accounts, then Signatures. Our step-by-step guides cover Gmail, Outlook and iPhone and Android.

Frequently asked questions

What should a teacher put in their email signature?

Your name as students and parents use it, your subject or grade, your school, and the best way to reach you, such as a school email and the school office number. Office hours or reply times are a useful extra.

Should teachers put their phone number in their email signature?

Use the school office number or your extension, not a personal mobile. A number in a signature travels with every forwarded email.

Can teachers use the school logo in their email signature?

Only if your school or district provides an approved logo for staff signatures. Otherwise leave it out and use the school name as text.

Should a teacher email signature include quotes?

Usually not. Many school communication policies discourage quotes, and they make every reply longer without helping parents reach you.

How long should a teacher email signature be?

Four or five lines is enough. Parents read most school email on their phones, so shorter signatures are easier to scan.
