How to Add an Email Signature on iPhone and Android

By Email Signature Generator · Published 9 October 2026 · 7 min read

The short answer: every mobile mail app keeps its own signature, separate from your desktop one. On iPhone Mail, go to Settings → Apps → Mail → Signature (Settings → Mail → Signature before iOS 18). In the Gmail app, open Menu → Settings → your account → Mobile signature. In Outlook mobile, open Settings → Signature. Keep the mobile version short and text-only: name, role, company, and one phone number or link.

Desktop signatures do not follow you to your phone. Each app (Apple Mail, Gmail, Outlook, Samsung Email) stores its own signature per device, and most mobile editors handle only basic text formatting. Plan for two versions: a full HTML signature on desktop and a compact text signature on mobile.

iPhone and iPad (Apple Mail)

  1. Open Settings.
  2. On iOS 18 or later, tap Apps, then Mail. On earlier versions, tap Mail directly.
  3. Scroll down and tap Signature.
  4. Choose All Accounts for one shared signature, or Per Account to set one for each address.
  5. Replace the default “Sent from my iPhone” text with your signature. Changes save as you type.
  6. Send a test message to an address you can open on desktop.

The iOS signature field supports bold, italic and underline. Pasting a styled HTML signature sometimes keeps colours and links, but images and table layouts are unreliable and may be stripped or sent as attachments. Treat any rich paste as an experiment and confirm it in a received message before relying on it.

Gmail app on Android, iPhone and iPad

Android

Open the Gmail app, tap Menu → Settings, choose the Google Account, then tap Mobile signature. Enter the text and tap OK. If you leave it empty, Google says new messages from the app use the signature you created on your computer.

iPhone and iPad

Open the Gmail app, tap Menu → Settings, then under Compose and Reply tap Signature settings. Turn on Mobile Signature, type it, and go back to save. Repeat for each account if you use several.

Gmail's mobile signature is plain text. For the full desktop setup, see how to add an email signature in Gmail.

Outlook mobile on iPhone and Android

Open Outlook, tap your profile picture or the menu, then the Settings gear, and tap Signature. If several accounts are connected, you can turn on a per-account signature. Formatting options vary by app version, so a short text signature is the safest choice. Outlook mobile signatures are separate from new Outlook, classic Outlook and Outlook on the web; see how to add an email signature in Outlook for those.

Samsung Email

In most versions, open Samsung Email, tap the menu (☰), then the Settings gear, select the account if asked, and tap Signature. Turn it on and edit the text. Menu names vary between One UI versions, so look for Signature under the account's settings if it is not at the top level.

What to put in a mobile signature

A phone screen shows a few lines at most, and recipients on mobile tap rather than read. Keep the mobile signature to the details someone needs to place you and reply:

Leave out images, social icons, long disclaimers you are not required to send, and “Sent from my iPhone”. If your role legally requires a disclaimer or licence number, include the short form on mobile too. Example details here are fictional.

Keep both versions consistent: use the same name, title and phone number on desktop and mobile, so a reply thread that mixes devices still looks like one person. Update both whenever your role or number changes.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't my email signature show up on my phone?

Mobile mail apps keep their own signature, separate from the one you set on a computer. Set it inside each app: iPhone Mail, Gmail, Outlook or Samsung Email.

Can I use an HTML signature with images on iPhone?

Not reliably. The iOS Mail signature field supports basic text formatting, and pasted images or table layouts may be stripped or sent as attachments. Use a short text signature on mobile and keep the HTML version for desktop.

How do I remove “Sent from my iPhone”?

Open Settings → Apps → Mail → Signature (Settings → Mail → Signature before iOS 18) and delete or replace the text.

What should a mobile email signature include?

Your name, role and company, and one contact route such as a phone number. Add one link only if it is the next step you want recipients to take.
