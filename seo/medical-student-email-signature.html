Medical Student Email Signature Examples

By Email Signature Generator · Updated 10 November 2026

The short answer: A medical student email signature should show your full name, MD Candidate or DO Candidate with your class year, your medical school, and your school email. Do not use Dr or put MD or DO after your name until you graduate, and make it clear you are a student when you email patients, preceptors or clinical staff.

Medical students email deans, residents, attendings, research mentors and residency programs. The signature has two jobs: say which school and class you are in, and make it plain that you are a student and not yet a physician.

Medical student email signature examples

What to include

  • Your full name.
  • MD Candidate or DO Candidate, followed by your class year: MD Candidate, Class of 2028.
  • Your medical school name in full.
  • Your school email address. Use it for anything related to clinical work.
  • A phone number during clinical years, when residents and coordinators need to reach you quickly.
  • A leadership or research role when you write in that capacity, such as a student organisation office or a named lab.
  • A degree you already hold, such as MPH or PhD, may follow your name.

What to leave out

  • Do not use Dr, MD or DO before graduation. It can mislead patients and staff about who is treating them.
  • Avoid MS3 or M2 when you write to people outside medicine. MS also means Master of Science. Write Third-Year Medical Student or your class year.
  • Do not add a hospital logo or name unless the hospital has told you to. You are a student of the school, not an employee of the site.
  • Do not add your own confidentiality notice. If your school or clinical site supplies one, use theirs word for word.
  • Leave out board scores, class rank and honours.

Title and degree wording on this page is general guidance. Your university, program or employer may set its own signature format, so check their style guide first.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write MD Candidate in an email signature?

Put it on the line under your name with your class year: MD Candidate, Class of 2028. Osteopathic students write DO Candidate. Put your medical school on the next line.

Can a medical student use Dr or Student Doctor in a signature?

Do not use Dr before you graduate. Some schools use Student Doctor on the wards, and some forbid it. In writing, Medical Student or MD Candidate is clearer, so use that unless your school tells you otherwise.

Should I put MS3 or my class year?

Use your class year, or write out Third-Year Medical Student. MS3 and M3 are understood inside medical schools, but outside them MS reads as Master of Science.

Do I need a HIPAA confidentiality notice?

Not one you write yourself. If your school or clinical site requires a notice, they will give you the wording. Either way, do not send patient information from a personal email account.
