Lawyer Email Signature Examples

By Email Signature Generator · Updated 25 October 2026

The short answer: A lawyer's email signature should show your name, title, firm, direct phone and office address, and your bar admissions if your jurisdiction expects them. A short confidentiality notice is common, but keep it under three lines and check your state's advertising rules, since some require extra wording on marketing emails.

Clients judge a firm by small details, and the signature is one of the few things they see on every email. A good lawyer's signature is formal, easy to read on a phone and short enough that the confidentiality notice does not outweigh the message.

Lawyer email signature examples

What to include

  • Your full name and title, such as Partner, Associate or Of Counsel.
  • Your firm name and office address, which many state bars expect on lawyer communications.
  • A direct phone line and your email.
  • Bar admissions or your bar number, where your jurisdiction or firm requires it.
  • A short confidentiality notice if your firm uses one.
  • Attorney Advertising wording on marketing emails, if your state requires it.

What to leave out

  • Do not use a ten-line disclaimer. Courts give boilerplate little weight, and clients stop reading.
  • Avoid inspirational quotes, emojis and bright colours. Formality is part of the message.
  • Do not claim to be a specialist or expert unless your state bar certifies you as one.
  • Leave out personal social accounts. Link to the firm's LinkedIn page if anything.

Licence, disclaimer and credential wording on this page is general information, not legal advice. Check the current rules with your regulator, employer or brokerage.

Frequently asked questions

Do lawyers need a disclaimer in their email signature?

Usually it is firm policy rather than a legal requirement. A short confidentiality notice is common practice, but courts give boilerplate little weight, so it does not replace care about who you email. Some states require Attorney Advertising wording on marketing emails, so check your state bar's rules.

Should I put my bar number in my email signature?

Some jurisdictions and firms expect bar admissions or a bar number on lawyer communications, and many lawyers list their admitted states. The generator's compliance fields add a bar number and state in small print under your details.

What is the best email signature format for a law firm?

A formal layout with name, title, firm, direct line, office address and one link to the firm's website. Use the firm's colour sparingly, avoid images other than the logo, and keep any notice to two or three lines.
