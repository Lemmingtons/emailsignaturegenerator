Real Estate Agent Email Signature Examples

By Email Signature Generator · Updated 25 October 2026

The short answer: A real estate agent's email signature should show your name, your licence number and brokerage where your state or brokerage requires them, one phone number, and a single booking or listings link. Add a short Equal Housing Opportunity line in the US, and leave out quotes, awards lists and more than three social icons.

Buyers and sellers read agent emails on their phones, often between viewings. A good signature makes the next step obvious, which is usually to call you or book a time, and carries the details your licence rules ask for without turning into a wall of small print.

Real estate agent email signature examples

What to include

  • Your full name and a title buyers understand, such as Sales Agent or Associate Broker.
  • Your licence number and state, if your state or brokerage requires it in advertising. Many do, and email can count as advertising.
  • Your brokerage name. Most brokerages require it on anything you send to clients.
  • One mobile number, written in full international format if you work with overseas buyers.
  • One call to action: book a viewing, see current listings, or get a free appraisal.
  • A short Equal Housing Opportunity line or logo in the US.

What to leave out

  • Use REALTOR® only if you are a member of the National Association of Realtors. It is a registered membership mark, not a job title.
  • Do not list every award, designation and sales figure. Pick the one a buyer will recognise.
  • Skip large listing photos in the signature. They get clipped in replies and blocked by many inboxes.
  • Do not use three different phone numbers. Clients will call the wrong one.

Licence, disclaimer and credential wording on this page is general information, not legal advice. Check the current rules with your regulator, employer or brokerage.

Frequently asked questions

Do real estate agents have to put their licence number in their email signature?

It depends on your state and your brokerage. Many US states require a licence number or brokerage name in advertising, and some treat email to the public as advertising. Check your state real estate commission's rules and your brokerage's policy. The generator's compliance fields add the licence number, state and brokerage under your signature.

Can I call myself a REALTOR® in my signature?

Only if you are a member of the National Association of Realtors. REALTOR® is a registered membership mark. If you are not a member, use a plain title such as Sales Agent, Real Estate Agent or Associate Broker.

Should I put listing photos in my email signature?

No. Large images are often blocked, clipped in replies or flagged as promotional. Link to your current listings with a button instead, and keep the signature to your headshot or brokerage logo.

What is the best call to action for a real estate signature?

Use one. Book a viewing, see my listings or get a free appraisal all work. More than one link competes for attention and none of them get clicked.
