Exclaimer or Email Signature Generator: the verdict

Pick Exclaimer if you run IT for an organisation and need signatures applied to every mailbox from one console. For one person or a small team it is the wrong shape: the ten-user minimum puts the smallest bill at about US$108 a year, and the free generator does not save anything. Here you pay US$9 once and keep your logo hosted.

When Exclaimer is the better choice

  • IT manages signatures for ten or more mailboxes and wants them applied automatically.
  • You need signatures added on the server, so they appear on every device and in replies.
  • You want banner campaigns, targeting rules or engagement analytics.

When this generator is the better choice

  • You need a signature for yourself or a small team and do not want a ten-seat minimum.
  • You would rather pay once than renew per mailbox every year.
  • You want your logo and colours pulled from your website instead of building a brand kit.

How to switch from Exclaimer

  1. If Exclaimer applies your signature server-side, ask your admin to remove you from the signature rule first, or you will get two signatures.
  2. Type your company website into the generator. Your logo, brand colours and social links are filled in.
  3. Pick a template, add your details and check the preview.
  4. Pay US$9 once, copy the signature and paste it into your email client.
  5. Send a test email to yourself and to a colleague to confirm only one signature appears.
