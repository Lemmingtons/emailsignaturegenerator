How to add your signature to Apple Mail

  1. Click "Copy for Gmail" in the generator
  2. Open Apple Mail → Preferences → Signatures
  3. Click "+" to add a new signature
  4. Uncheck "Always match my default message font"
  5. Click inside the signature editor, press Cmd+A then Cmd+V
