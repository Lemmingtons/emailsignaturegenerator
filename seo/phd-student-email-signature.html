PhD Student Email Signature Examples

By Email Signature Generator · Updated 10 November 2026

The short answer: A PhD student email signature should show your full name, PhD Student or PhD Candidate with your field, your department and university, and your university email. Write Candidate only after you have advanced to candidacy, and do not use Dr or put PhD after your name until the degree is awarded.

Doctoral students write to supervisors, journal editors, conference organisers and researchers they have never met. The signature is often the only introduction they get, so it should say what you research and where, without claiming a title you do not hold yet.

PhD student email signature examples

What to include

  • Your full name, matching the name you publish under.
  • PhD Student or PhD Candidate, followed by your field.
  • Your department, then the university.
  • Your lab or research group when you write to people outside your department.
  • Your university email address.
  • One research link: your lab page, a personal academic site, Google Scholar or your ORCID record.
  • Your teaching or research assistant title when you write in that role.

What to leave out

  • Do not use Dr or put PhD after your name before the degree is awarded.
  • Do not write PhD Candidate before you have advanced to candidacy. At most US universities that means passing qualifying exams and having a proposal approved. Until then, write PhD Student.
  • ABD (all but dissertation) is informal. Leave it out of a signature.
  • Do not list publications, grants or awards. Link to a page that has them.
  • Skip quotes and long lists of pronoun, land or availability statements unless your department asks for them.

Title and degree wording on this page is general guidance. Your university, program or employer may set its own signature format, so check their style guide first.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between PhD Student and PhD Candidate in a signature?

A PhD student is enrolled in a doctoral program. A PhD candidate has advanced to candidacy, which at most US universities means finishing coursework, passing qualifying exams and having a dissertation proposal approved. Use the title that matches your current status, and check how your program defines candidacy.

Can I put PhD(c) or ABD after my name?

It is better not to. PhD(c) and ABD are informal and many academics read them as overstating your status. Write PhD Candidate on the title line instead.

Should I include my ORCID or Google Scholar link?

Yes, if you have published or are submitting work. Use one research link, not three. An ORCID record or a personal academic site is the most stable choice because it stays the same when you change university.

When can I start using Dr in my email signature?

After your degree has been conferred. Some people start after a successful defence and final submission, but the safe rule is to wait for the university to award the degree.
