Frequently Asked Questions

Can Canva make an HTML email signature?

Not at the moment. On 20 November 2026 Canva's help page said exporting as HTML is not yet available and recommended saving the signature as a PNG image.

Why is an image-only email signature a problem?

The text cannot be selected or copied, links do not work unless you add them by hand, and many email clients block images until the reader allows them, so the signature can appear blank.

Is Canva free for email signatures?

Canva's email signature templates and editor are free. Canva Pro, listed at US$18 a month for one person on 20 November 2026, adds more Brand Kits: five instead of one.

Can I keep my Canva design style?

You can get close. Pick a template here, set your brand colours (or let the website import fill them in), choose from email-safe fonts and add your logo. If your Canva design has a graphic element, you can include it as an image in the signature.
