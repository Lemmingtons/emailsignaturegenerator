What the checker actually tests

Gmail dark-mode safety

Flags pure-black text, missing backgrounds, and mid-tone greys Gmail silently recolours in dark inboxes.

WCAG AA contrast

Calculates real contrast ratios between every text/background pair. Fails anything under 4.5:1.

Outlook compatibility

Detects display:flex, embedded <style> blocks, and other patterns Outlook strips.

Tracker pixels

Detects 1×1 images and known tracker domains. These degrade deliverability and recipients' filters block them.

Size & weight

Warns above 10KB, fails above 25KB. Gmail clips messages over 102KB per thread.

Accessibility

Missing alt text, layout tables without role="presentation", link text, and more.

How to turn the report into fixes

1. Repair anything critical

Fix invisible text, broken image URLs, invalid links, and layouts that rely on flexbox, grid, or external stylesheets before changing the design.

2. Reduce signature weight

Compress photos, avoid base64 image data, remove tracking pixels, and keep HTML compact so Gmail does not clip long threads.

3. Check real inboxes

Send a test email to Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail. Check desktop, mobile, light mode, and dark mode before giving the signature to a team.

Example issues the checker finds

Local image path

C:\Users\Jane\logo.png may display on your computer but fails for recipients. Use a public HTTPS image URL instead.

Low-contrast footer text

Light grey disclaimers can drop below WCAG AA contrast and become unreadable in dark inboxes or on mobile screens.

Outlook-hostile CSS

CSS grid and flexbox are common in web pages, but classic Outlook for Windows can strip or misread them inside emails.

Frequently asked questions

What is an email signature checker?

A signature checker reads your existing HTML and flags issues that would cause it to render poorly in certain email clients. Our free checker tests contrast ratios, Gmail dark-mode compatibility, Outlook rendering, tracker pixels, accessibility, and total file size.

Is this signature checker free?

Yes. The health check is completely free and runs entirely in your browser — your signature HTML is never uploaded or stored. There is no account or signup.

How do I test if my email signature works in dark mode?

Paste your signature HTML into our free health check. It scans for the specific patterns that break Gmail dark mode — pure #000000 text, missing background-colour declarations, and mid-tone greys that Gmail silently recolours. You'll see a full report in under a second.

What does the signature score mean?

The score is out of 100. Each audit check can deduct points: critical issues (invisible text, broken layouts) cost 20, warnings cost 7, info-level nits cost 2. 90+ is excellent, 75–89 is good, below 50 indicates a signature that will likely break for a significant share of recipients.

Do you store the HTML I paste?

No. The entire audit runs in your browser using the built-in DOMParser. Nothing is sent to a server, nothing is stored anywhere. Close the tab and it's gone.
