Frequently asked questions

What is the best free email signature generator?

HubSpot's generator is the best fully free option: no account, no paid plan and HTML you can copy. Its limits are a small template set and no way to pull in your logo from your website, so you need your logo hosted at a public image URL first.

Is there an email signature generator without a subscription?

Yes. HubSpot's generator is free with no plan at all. Our generator is a one-time payment of US$9 with no renewal. Most other tools, including WiseStamp, MySignature, Newoldstamp, Exclaimer and CodeTwo, are subscriptions, and some stop your signature working when the subscription ends.

Which email signature generator can use my company website?

Our generator is the only one we found that documents filling in your logo, brand colours, company name, phone and social links from a typed website address. Other tools ask you to upload a logo or paste an image URL and choose colours by hand. Sites that block automated requests may still need manual entry.

Do I need a signature management platform or just a generator?

A generator is enough for individuals and small teams who install their own signatures. Choose a management platform such as Exclaimer or CodeTwo when IT needs to apply and update signatures for every mailbox centrally, or when legal wording must be enforced.

Are image-based email signatures a good idea?

Usually not. Many email clients block images until the recipient allows them, so an image-only signature can arrive blank or as an attachment, and the text cannot be selected or tapped. HTML signatures with real text and a small logo are more reliable.
