Graduate Student Email Signature Examples

By Email Signature Generator · Updated 10 November 2026

The short answer: A graduate student email signature should show your full name, your degree and field written as a candidate or student (for example MS Candidate, Mechanical Engineering), your department and university, and one way to reach you. Add your assistantship title if you email as a TA or RA, and leave the degree letters off your name until the degree is awarded.

Grad students email professors, students they teach, collaborators at other universities and employers, sometimes in the same afternoon. A good signature tells each of them what you study and in what capacity you are writing, in three or four lines.

Graduate student email signature examples

What to include

  • Your full name, as it appears on your university record or the name you publish under.
  • Your degree and field, written as a student or candidate: MS Candidate, Mechanical Engineering, or Master of Public Health student.
  • Your department or school, then the university name.
  • Your assistantship title when you write in that role, such as Graduate Teaching Assistant or Graduate Research Assistant.
  • Your university email address. Add a phone number only if you want calls from the people you are writing to.
  • One link that helps the reader: LinkedIn for job searches, or a lab or portfolio page for research contacts.
  • Your expected graduation date when you are applying for jobs or internships.

What to leave out

  • Do not put MS, MA or MBA after your name until the degree has been awarded. Before that, write Candidate or student.
  • Leave out your GPA, coursework and awards. Those belong on your resume.
  • Skip inspirational quotes and personal social media accounts.
  • Check your university brand rules before using the logo or seal. Many universities limit them to official communications.
  • Do not list every role you hold. Use the one that matches the email you are sending.

Title and degree wording on this page is general guidance. Your university, program or employer may set its own signature format, so check their style guide first.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a master's degree in progress in my email signature?

Write the degree as a candidate or student, followed by your field: MS Candidate, Computer Science, or Master of Social Work student. Do not put the degree letters after your name until the degree has been awarded.

Should I list both my TA and RA roles?

No. Use the role you are writing in. Use the teaching assistant title when you email students in your course, and the research assistant title when you email collaborators. Most email clients let you save more than one signature.

Should a graduate student email signature include a phone number?

Only if you want the people you email to call you. For most academic email, your university address is enough. Add a mobile number when you are job hunting or coordinating fieldwork.

Can I include my bachelor's degree after my name?

You can, but it is unusual in the United States. Most graduate students list only the degree they are working toward. If you hold a professional licence or certification that matters to the reader, that is more useful than a bachelor's degree.
