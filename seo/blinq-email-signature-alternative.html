Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Blinq email signature an image?

Outside Blinq's Business plans, yes. On 20 November 2026 Blinq's help centre said it generates an image of your signature with an embedded link to your signature card, and that HTML signatures are available on Business plans.

How much does Blinq cost?

On 20 November 2026 Blinq listed Premium at US$9.99 a month or US$7.33 a month billed annually, and Business at US$4.99 per card a month billed annually from five cards. A free tier was also listed. Check their pricing page for current figures.

Can I keep my Blinq business card and use this signature?

Yes. Add your Blinq card link to the signature as a button or link. The signature itself stays HTML, so the rest of your details work without the card.

Does the signature work on phones?

A signature set in Gmail or Outlook on the web or desktop is added when you send from there. The Gmail and Outlook phone apps keep their own plain-text signature, which Blinq also notes, so set a short one there. The phone guide on this site covers iPhone and Android.
