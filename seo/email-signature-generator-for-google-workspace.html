How to add your signature to Google Workspace

  1. Click <strong>"Copy for Gmail"</strong> in the generator
  2. Open <strong>Gmail</strong> in your Google Workspace account
  3. Click the gear icon → <strong>"See all settings"</strong>
  4. Scroll to the <strong>"Signature"</strong> section and create a new signature
  5. Paste with <code>Ctrl+V</code> and save
