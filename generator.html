How to add this signature to Gmail
  1. Click "Copy HTML" above
  2. Open Gmail and click the gear icon (top right)
  3. Click "See all settings"
  4. Scroll to the "Signature" section
  5. Click "Create new" and give it a name
  6. Click inside the editor, press Ctrl+A (or Cmd+A) then Ctrl+V (Cmd+V)
  7. Scroll down and click "Save Changes"
  8. Set it as your default for new emails and replies
How to add this signature to Outlook
  1. Click "Copy HTML" above (works for Outlook too)
  2. Open Outlook → Settings → Compose and reply
  3. Under Email signature, click "+ New signature"
  4. Paste with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V)
  5. Click Save and set as default
